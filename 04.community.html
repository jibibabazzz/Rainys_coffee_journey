COMMUNITY

Coffee Events & Community Updates

This page shares coffee community updates, events, and home barista showcases. It connects coffee learning with real coffee culture.

Upcoming Events

EVENT • MELBOURNE CBD

Melbourne Coffee Expo

A large coffee exhibition and networking event in Melbourne featuring coffee brands, roasting equipment, brewing tools, sensory workshops, and industry discussions. Visitors can explore new coffee trends, taste specialty coffee, and learn from professionals.

WORKSHOP • BRUNSWICK EAST

Latte Art Workshop by ONA

A beginner-friendly workshop introducing milk steaming, pouring techniques, and simple latte art patterns such as hearts and tulips. Participants can practice with professional coffee equipment and learn café workflows.

Community Showcase

CUPPING • FITZROY

Weekly Cupping at Rosso Coffee

A weekly coffee tasting session focused on flavour notes, aroma,acidity, sweetness, and coffee evaluation techniques. This activity helps coffee enthusiasts improve sensory knowledge and tasting skills.

BAR TAKEOVER • MELBOURNE

Recovery Coffee Guest Takeover at PATH

A collaborative coffee event between Korean specialty coffee guests and Melbourne café culture. Visitors can experience unique brewing styles, signature drinks, and international coffee perspectives.

CUPPING • SOUTH MELBOURNE

Specialty Tasting at ST ALi

A community coffee tasting session introducing seasonal beans, brewing recipes, and roasting profiles. Participants can learn about coffee origin and flavour balance through guided tasting.

POP-UP • CARLTON

Beans and Folks Pop-Up Coffee Meeting

A casual coffee community event focused on seasonal espresso, filter coffee tasting, and conversations between coffee lovers, baristas, and home brewers.
